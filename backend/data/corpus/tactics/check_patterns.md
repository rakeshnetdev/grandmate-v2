## Fast Checkmate Patterns

Common fast checkmate patterns and tactical motifs that often lead to losing the queen
in the opening or early middlegame.

| Pattern | Typical sequence | Main idea |
|---|---|---|
| Fool's Mate | `1. f3 e5 2. g4 Qh4#` | The fastest possible mate, created by weakening the diagonal to the king. |
| Scholar's Mate | `1. e4 e5 2. Qh5 Nc6 3. Bc4 Nf6?? 4. Qxf7#` | Queen and bishop combine to attack the weak f7 square. |
| Scholar's Mate setup variation | `1. e4 e5 2. Bc4 Nc6 3. Qf3` | Another common setup aiming at f7, often seen in beginner games. |
| Wayward Queen Attack | `1. e4 e5 2. Qh5` | An early queen sortie that pressures f7 and can transpose into Scholar's Mate ideas. |
| Back-rank Mate | No single fixed sequence; usually a rook or queen mates on the back rank after the king is trapped by its own pawns | The defending king has no escape squares because its own pawns block flight squares. |
| Smothered Mate | No single fixed sequence; often a knight delivers mate when the king is boxed in by its own pieces | The king is trapped by friendly pieces and cannot escape a knight check. |
